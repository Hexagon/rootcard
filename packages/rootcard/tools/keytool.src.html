rootcard key tool

The tool for everything that needs your recovery card: moving an identity, resetting or adding a password, rotating the recovery key and closing the identity. It runs entirely in this page and talks only to the hosts you enter.

Only enter your recovery key in the rootcard key tool, never in an application. Save this page and open it from your own disk.
